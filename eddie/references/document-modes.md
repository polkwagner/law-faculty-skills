# Document Modes

## Email

Prioritize clear ask, audience fit, authority boundaries, dates, attachments, and concise action items. Apply project greeting and closing conventions only when the message actually includes them.

## Memo, Proposal, and Report

Prioritize support for recommendations, role boundaries, factual claims, source integrity, plan alignment, and whether the conclusion arrives early enough for the intended reader.

## Academic Writing

Prioritize quotation fidelity, source-to-proposition fit, attribution, temporal claims, methodological claims, and distinctions between evidence and inference.

## Slides

Prioritize statements that a speaker may present as fact, citation legibility, numerical consistency, audience-sensitive language, and whether slide brevity has created an unsupported implication.

## Static Website

Prioritize public/private boundaries, current labels and dates, deep links, external destinations, heading hierarchy, accessible link text, durable versus temporary content, and consistency with linked sites. Do not claim visual or technical accessibility compliance without an appropriate rendering or audit tool.

## Other

State which checks apply and which do not before reviewing. Do not force an artifact into email, memo, or website conventions merely because those are familiar checklists.
